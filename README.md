# Superstore Sales Analysis and Interactive Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data to understand business performance, sales trends, profitability, product categories and regional performance.

An interactive dashboard was developed using Tableau to present key business metrics and insights through different visualizations.

## Problem Statement
The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify trends, patterns and opportunities for business improvement. The analysis helps understand which cities, regions and product categories contribute most to sales and profit.

## Dataset Information

## Key Business Insights
1. Technology has the highest sales among the three product categories.
2. The West region generates the highest profit among the four regions.
3. New York City has the highest sales among the displayed cities.
4. Chairs show strong sales performance among the displayed sub-categories.
5. November records the highest monthly sales in the displayed trend.
